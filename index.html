How the gauges work
Speed
The share of all your problems that you've solved. 10 of 40 solved reads 25%.
Fuel
Of the problems you've solved, the share counted as solved without hints. Every tick counts as no hints by default. If you peeked at a solution, open the row with the arrow and untick "Solved without hints" to burn some fuel.
Streak
Days in a row with at least one solve, out of 30. Missing a whole day resets it to 0, and today is still safe until midnight.
Exits
Topics you've learned, out of 14.
